Moora AI

面向全球书法爱好者的 AI 书法成长与创作软件An AI calligraphy growth and creation app for enthusiasts worldwide

围绕真实纸面书写，提供内容推荐、个性化练习、创作支持、作品点评、作品档案与书友社区，把「看到内容 → 决定写法 → 真实书写 → 获得反馈 → 保存记录 → 再次书写」连成完整闭环。Moora 不是在生成内容，而是在为每一个长期书写者建立一份持续生长的个人书写史。

Built around real paper-and-ink writing, Moora connects content discovery, personalised practice, creation support, work critique, portfolio archiving and a calligrapher community into a single loop—"see content, decide how to write, actually write, get feedback, keep the record, write again." It is not generating content; it is building a personal writing history that keeps growing for every long-term writer.

竞争位置Competitive Position

传统字帖工具围绕单次任务，AI 书法产品解决单次点评，通用大模型并非书法专用，真人老师难以覆盖每一次日常书写。Traditional tools handle single tasks; AI calligraphy products handle one-off critique; general-purpose LLMs are not calligraphy-specific; human teachers cannot cover every daily session.

Moora 的差异化在「长期过程 × 理解用户」：用户书写次数越多，产品对这个人的判断越准。Moora sits at "long-term process × understanding the user": the more a user writes, the better the product understands them.
